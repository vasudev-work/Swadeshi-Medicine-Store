Legal, privacy and terms

Last updated 4 November 2026. This page describes exactly how this build behaves — not how a typical app behaves.

Privacy policy

This is a static web page. It has no backend, no database and no user accounts.

  • There is no sign-up, no login and no profile.
  • There is no analytics, no tag manager, no pixel and no third-party script of any kind.
  • Nothing is loaded from a CDN — no web fonts, no icon packs, no trackers. Every asset is inside the page itself.
  • Your saved list, theme choice, accent colour and a random local id are stored in this browser's localStorage under the key swadeshi.v1. They stay on your device and are not transmitted anywhere.
  • Search runs on the device against a product list embedded in the page. Your search text is never sent to a server.
  • The only data that leaves your device is an email you compose and send yourself, through your own mail application, when you report a listing or submit a medicine. That email goes to the operator's address and contains what you typed.
  • Clearing your browser data deletes everything this page stored.

Because there is no server, the operator cannot see your saved list, your searches or your theme — and cannot restore them if you clear your browser.

Terms of use

  • This page is an information and price-lookup tool built on the public Jan Aushadhi (PMBJP) product list dated 13 January 2023.
  • It is not a pharmacy, does not sell medicines, does not take orders and does not process payments.
  • It gives no medical advice. Doses, substitutions and suitability are decisions for a registered medical practitioner or pharmacist. Always follow your doctor's prescription.
  • Prices shown are the MRP printed in the source list. They can change, and availability differs by Jan Aushadhi Kendra.
  • Manufacturer names, batch numbers, stock and expiry are not part of the source list and are therefore not shown. They are never guessed.
  • Brand names mentioned on the page belong to their respective owners and are used only to point to the generic equivalent.

Refunds and cancellations

There is nothing to buy here, so there is nothing to refund and nothing to cancel. No payment is collected, no card details are requested, and no order is created. Any purchase happens at a physical Jan Aushadhi Kendra under that store's own terms.

Cookies

This page sets no cookies. It stores preferences in localStorage, which is not a cookie and is never sent with network requests. There are no advertising or analytics cookies because there are no advertisers or analytics.

Accessibility

  • Interactive controls are real <button> and <a> elements, reachable and operable by keyboard.
  • Focus is always visible, with a 2px focus ring on every control.
  • Tap targets are at least 44px tall on touch layouts.
  • Text contrast targets 4.5:1 or better in both the dark and light themes.
  • Sheets are dialogs with aria-modal, a labelled title, and Escape to close.
  • Motion respects prefers-reduced-motion.
  • Colour is never the only signal: saved state also changes the button's accessible name.

If something is hard to use with a screen reader, keyboard or zoom, write to the operator and it gets fixed.

Security

  • No server, no database and no credentials exist behind this page, so there is no data store to breach.
  • All product data is embedded at build time; nothing is fetched at runtime.
  • The page is served as static files. Serving it over HTTPS is recommended and is what GitHub Pages does by default.
  • Reports and submissions are ordinary emails, handled by your mail provider and the operator's mail provider.

Responsible disclosure

If you find a security or privacy problem, email the operator with the steps to reproduce it and what you expected instead. Please do not open a public issue with a working exploit, and do not test against data that is not yours. There is no bug bounty; there is a thank-you and a fix.
